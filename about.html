About this site

Bone Explorer is a free, kid-friendly way to explore all 206 bones in the human body. It was made for curious kids around ages 6 to 9, and for the grown-ups exploring with them.

🦴 What you can do

  • Zoom in close and tap any bone, even the three tiny bones inside your ear.
  • Read what each bone does, a fun fact, and something to try on your own body.
  • Search by everyday or real names, like “kneecap” or “patella”.
  • Play the quiz at three levels, and have everything read out loud.

👋 Who made it

Bone Explorer was made by Nicolas Nowinski. The bone drawings are made from code instead of image files, so every bone stays sharp no matter how far you zoom in.

💻 Source code

Bone Explorer is open source under the MIT License. You can read the code, report a problem, or make your own copy on GitHub.

See it on GitHub ➜

🔒 Privacy

There are no accounts, ads or trackers. The bones you’ve found and your read-aloud setting are saved only in your own browser. Read-aloud uses the voices built into your device. Fonts come from Google Fonts.

🩺 A note for grown-ups

The drawings are simplified so kids can find each bone easily. Bone Explorer is for learning and fun, not for medical advice.
